## Approving/Rejecting Bookings

### Step-by-Step: Approve a Booking

1. **Log into Admin Dashboard**
   - Go to: https://facetraash-bfhgt67h.manus.space/admin
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click "Sign In"

2. **Find Pending Bookings**
   - Look for "Booking Requests" section
   - Filter by "Pending" status
   - You'll see a list of new booking requests

3. **Review Booking Details**
   - Click on a booking to view full details
   - Check: Customer name, email, phone, service type, date, location
   - Verify the zip code is in your service area
   - Read any customer notes

4. **Approve the Booking**
   - Click the "Approve" button (green button)
   - (Optional) Add approval notes for customer
   - Click "Confirm Approval"
   - Customer receives approval email automatically

5. **Next Steps**
   - Add booking to your scheduling system
   - Prepare for service delivery
   - Contact customer if needed

### Step-by-Step: Reject a Booking

1. **Log into Admin Dashboard**
   - Go to: https://facetraash-bfhgt67h.manus.computer/admin
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click "Sign In"

2. **Find Pending Bookings**
   - Look for "Booking Requests" section
   - Filter by "Pending" status

3. **Review Booking Details**
   - Click on a booking to view full details
   - Verify why you're rejecting (e.g., outside service area, date unavailable)

4. **Reject the Booking**
   - Click the "Reject" button (red button)
   - Add rejection reason (customer will see this)
   - Click "Confirm Rejection"
   - Customer receives rejection email automatically

5. **Follow Up (Optional)**
   - Call customer to explain (if outside service area, suggest alternatives)
   - Offer to help them reschedule for a different date

### Booking Status Reference

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Pending** | Awaiting your approval | Review and approve/reject |
| **Approved** | Confirmed with customer | Add to schedule |
| **Rejected** | Declined by you | Customer notified |
| **Completed** | Service delivered | Archive booking |

## Responding to Contact Submissions

### Step-by-Step: Respond to Contact Form

1. **Log into Admin Dashboard**
   - Go to: https://facetraash-bfhgt67h.manus.space/admin
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click "Sign In"

2. **Find Contact Submissions**
   - Look for "Contact Submissions" section
   - You'll see a list of customer inquiries

3. **Review Submission**
   - Click on a submission to view full details
   - See: Customer name, email, phone, message, date submitted

4. **Respond to Customer**
   - Option A: Reply via email
     - Copy customer email address
     - Send email from your email address (Taylorwilson59@facehaulingservices.com)
     - Reference their submission in your reply
   
   - Option B: Call customer
     - Use phone number from submission
     - Reference their inquiry
     - Provide helpful information

5. **Archive Submission**
   - Click "Archive" to mark as handled
   - (Optional) Add notes about your response

### Contact Form Fields

- **Name:** Customer's full name
- **Email:** Customer's email address
- **Phone:** Customer's phone number
- **Service Type:** Type of service they're interested in
- **Message:** Customer's question or inquiry

### Response Time Target

- **Ideal:** Within 1 business day
- **Maximum:** Within 2 business days
- **Urgent:** If customer mentions emergency, call immediately

### Viewing Payment Status

**In Admin Dashboard:**
1. Go to Admin Dashboard
2. Look for "Payments" section
3. View: Payment amount, date, customer, status
4. Click payment to see details

### Payment Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Payment in progress |
| **Completed** | Payment successful |
| **Failed** | Payment declined |
| **Refunded** | Payment refunded to customer |

### Refunding a Payment

**To Refund a Customer:**
1. Go to Admin Dashboard → Payments
2. Find payment to refund
3. Click "Refund" button
4. Enter refund amount (full or partial)
5. Add reason for refund
6. Click "Confirm Refund"
7. Customer receives refund within 3-5 business days

1. Claim your Stripe sandbox (link in email)
2. Complete Stripe KYC verification
3. Switch to Live Mode in Manus settings
4. Enter live API keys
5. Test with real card
6. Go live!

### Payment Methods Accepted

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- Debit cards
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay

## Viewing Analytics

### Website Traffic

**To View Website Traffic:**
1. Contact Manus support at https://help.manus.im
2. Ask: "Can you enable analytics for my website?"
3. Manus will provide: Dashboard link and login credentials
4. View: Visitors, page views, bounce rate, traffic sources

### What Analytics Show

- **Visitors:** Number of unique visitors
- **Page Views:** Total pages viewed
- **Bounce Rate:** % of visitors who leave without taking action
- **Traffic Sources:** Where visitors came from (Google, social media, direct)
- **Top Pages:** Most visited pages
- **Device Type:** Desktop, mobile, tablet

### Using Analytics to Improve

- **High bounce rate?** Update page content or improve design
- **Low traffic?** Increase social media marketing
- **Visitors not converting?** Make "Request a Quote" button more prominent
- **Mobile traffic low?** Ensure mobile design is good

## Troubleshooting Common Problems

### Problem: Booking form won't submit

**Possible Causes:**
1. Missing required fields (marked with *)
2. Date is in the past
3. Zip code not in service area
4. Browser cache issue
5. Internet connection problem

**Solutions:**
1. Fill all required fields completely
2. Select a date in the future
3. Use zip code lookup tool to verify service area
4. Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
5. Restart internet connection
6. Try a different browser

### Problem: Customer not receiving confirmation email

**Possible Causes:**
1. Email address entered incorrectly
2. Email in spam/junk folder
3. Email server delay
4. Email bounced (invalid address)

**Solutions:**
1. Check email address in booking form
2. Ask customer to check spam/junk folder
3. Wait 10-15 minutes for email to arrive
4. Manually send customer an email
5. Call customer to confirm booking

### Problem: Admin dashboard won't load

**Possible Causes:**
1. Not logged in
2. Browser cache issue
3. Internet connection problem
4. Server issue

**Solutions:**
1. Log in with email and password
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Restart internet connection
5. Wait 5-10 minutes
6. Contact Manus support if issue persists

### Problem: Website is very slow

**Possible Causes:**
1. Slow internet connection
2. Browser has too many tabs open
3. Browser cache is full
4. Server is busy

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet speed (speedtest.net)
2. Close unnecessary browser tabs
3. Clear browser cache
4. Wait a few minutes and try again
5. Try a different browser
6. Contact Manus support if issue persists

### Problem: Payment won't process

**Possible Causes:**
1. Card declined
2. Card expired
3. Incorrect card details
4. Stripe is in test mode
5. Internet connection issue

**Solutions:**
1. Try a different payment method
2. Verify card is not expired
3. Check card details are correct
4. Verify Stripe is in live mode (not test mode)
5. Check internet connection
6. Contact Manus support if issue persists

### Problem: Social media links not working

**Possible Causes:**
1. Social media page doesn't exist
2. URL is incorrect
3. Social media account is private
4. Link is broken

**Solutions:**
1. Verify social media page exists
2. Check URL is correct
3. Make social media account public
4. Contact Manus support to fix link
