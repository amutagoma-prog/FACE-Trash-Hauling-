## Module 1: Approving Quotes

### Overview

Customers submit quote requests through the website. Your job is to review them and decide whether to approve or reject them. When you approve, the customer automatically receives an email and SMS confirmation.

### Step-by-Step: Approving a Quote

**Step 1: Navigate to Bookings**

Click **"Bookings"** in the left sidebar.

You'll see a list of all bookings. Each booking shows:
- Customer name
- Service type (Residential, Commercial, etc.)
- Pickup date
- Status (Awaiting Review, Approved, Rejected)

**Step 2: Find Bookings Awaiting Review**

Look for bookings with status **"Awaiting Review"**

These are the ones that need your decision. You can also filter by status:
- Click the **"Status"** dropdown
- Select **"Awaiting Review"**
- Click **"Filter"**

**Step 3: Open a Booking**

Click on a booking in the list to open its details.

You'll see:
- **Customer Information** - Name, email, phone
- **Service Details** - Type, date, location
- **Containers** - Sizes and quantities requested
- **Submission Date** - When customer submitted
- **Approval Notes** - Space to add your notes

**Step 4: Review the Information**

Carefully review all details:

- Is the service type something you offer?
- Is the pickup date feasible?
- Do you have the equipment available?
- Is the location in your service area?
- Is the phone number valid?

**Step 5: Add Approval Notes (Optional)**

Before approving, you can add notes such as:
- "Approved - Scheduled for Monday 9:00 AM"
- "Approved - Will call to confirm time"
- "Approved - Standard residential pickup"

Click in the **"Approval Notes"** field and type your note.

**Step 6: Click Approve Quote**

Once you've reviewed everything, click the **"Approve Quote"** button (green button).

A confirmation dialog will appear asking "Are you sure you want to approve this quote?"

Click **"Confirm"** to proceed.

**Step 7: Confirmation**

You'll see a success message: "Quote approved successfully!"

The system automatically:
- Sends an email to the customer with approval and booking details
- Sends an SMS to the customer confirming approval
- Updates the booking status to "Approved"
- Notifies you of the action

The customer can now see their booking in their portal and track its status.

### Step-by-Step: Rejecting a Quote

**Step 1-3:** Same as approval (navigate to Bookings, filter, open booking)

**Step 4: Click Reject Quote**

Click the **"Reject Quote"** button (red button).

A dialog will appear asking for a rejection reason.

**Step 5: Enter Rejection Reason**

Provide a clear reason why you're rejecting the quote. Examples:

- "Outside our service area"
- "Equipment not available for requested date"
- "Service type not available"
- "Location too far from base"
- "Invalid contact information"

**Step 6: Click Confirm**

Click **"Confirm Rejection"**

**Step 7: Confirmation**

You'll see a success message: "Quote rejected successfully!"

The system automatically:
- Sends an email to the customer with the rejection reason
- Sends an SMS to the customer notifying them of rejection
- Updates the booking status to "Rejected"
- Notifies you of the action

The customer will see their booking status as "Rejected" in their portal.

### Booking Status Timeline

Customers see a visual timeline of their booking status:

```
Submitted → Awaiting Review → Approved/Rejected
```

If approved, it updates to:

```
Submitted → Awaiting Review → Approved → Scheduled → Completed
```

## Module 2: Managing Inventory

### Overview

Your equipment inventory (containers, trucks, tools) is critical for fulfilling bookings. You need to keep accurate counts so you know what's available.

### Step-by-Step: Viewing Inventory

**Step 1: Navigate to Inventory**

Click **"Inventory"** in the left sidebar.

You'll see three categories:
- **Containers** (96 gallon, 2-yard, 3-yard, 4-yard)
- **Trucks** (waste haulers, recycling trucks, etc.)
- **Tools** (rakes, shovels, equipment)

**Step 2: Review Current Quantities**

Each item shows:
- **Name** - Equipment description
- **Type** - Category (Container/Truck/Tool)
- **Quantity** - Number available
- **Status** - Active/Inactive
- **Last Updated** - When quantity was last changed

**Step 3: Identify Low Stock**

Look for items with low quantities. The dashboard may show a warning badge if any equipment is below threshold.

### Step-by-Step: Updating Equipment Quantity

**Step 1: Click on Equipment Item**

Find the equipment you want to update and click on it.

A detail panel will open showing:
- Current quantity
- Equipment name and type
- Status
- Last update date

**Step 2: Click Edit**

Click the **"Edit"** button.

The quantity field becomes editable.

**Step 3: Update Quantity**

Clear the current quantity and enter the new number.

Example: If you had 5 containers and just purchased 3 more, change from 5 to 8.

**Step 4: Add Update Note (Optional)**

In the **"Note"** field, explain why you're updating:
- "Purchased 3 new 4-yard containers"
- "2 containers in for repair"
- "Sold 1 truck to another company"
- "Inventory count correction"

**Step 5: Click Save**

Click the **"Save"** button.

You'll see a confirmation message: "Equipment updated successfully!"

The quantity is now updated and the change is logged.

### Equipment Status Codes

| Status | Meaning | Use When |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Active** | Equipment is available for use | Equipment is working and can be deployed |
| **Inactive** | Equipment is not available | Equipment is broken, in repair, or retired |
| **Reserved** | Equipment is reserved for specific booking | Equipment is allocated to a customer |

### Changing Equipment Status

**Step 1:** Click on equipment item  
**Step 2:** Click **"Edit"**  
**Step 3:** Change status dropdown  
**Step 4:** Click **"Save"**  

### Adding New Equipment

**Step 1: Click "Add Equipment"**

Click the **"Add Equipment"** button (usually at top of inventory list).

**Step 2: Enter Equipment Details**

Fill in:
- **Name** - Equipment description (e.g., "4-Yard Container")
- **Type** - Select from dropdown (Container/Truck/Tool)
- **Quantity** - Number of units
- **Status** - Active or Inactive

**Step 3: Click Create**

Click **"Create Equipment"**

The new equipment is added to your inventory.

### Best Practices for Inventory Management

**Daily:**
- Check inventory levels each morning
- Update quantities after each job
- Note any equipment issues

**Weekly:**
- Review low-stock items
- Plan equipment purchases
- Check for items needing repair

**Monthly:**
- Full inventory count
- Verify quantities match physical equipment
- Update status of broken equipment
- Plan for seasonal needs

## Module 3: Tracking Notifications

### Overview

The system automatically sends SMS and email notifications to customers. You can track the delivery status to ensure customers receive important updates.

### Step-by-Step: Viewing SMS Logs

**Step 1: Navigate to SMS Logs**

Click **"SMS Logs"** in the left sidebar.

You'll see a list of all SMS messages sent with:
- **Date Sent** - When message was sent
- **Phone Number** - Recipient's number
- **Message** - Content of SMS
- **Status** - Delivery status
- **Booking ID** - Which booking it relates to

**Step 2: Review Status Codes**

Each message shows a status:

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Pending** | Message queued, waiting to send | Wait a few minutes |
| **Sent** | Message sent to carrier | Normal - message is in transit |
| **Delivered** | Message delivered to phone | Success - customer received it |
| **Failed** | Message failed to send | Check phone number, try resending |
| **Bounced** | Message bounced back | Invalid number or carrier issue |

**Step 3: Filter by Date or Booking**

You can filter SMS logs:
- **By Date** - Click date range to filter
- **By Booking ID** - Search for specific booking
- **By Status** - Filter by delivery status

**Step 4: Troubleshoot Failed Messages**

If a message shows "Failed":

1. Check the phone number format (should be +1-XXX-XXX-XXXX)
2. Verify the number is correct
3. Try resending the message
4. Contact customer to verify phone number
5. Update phone number if incorrect

### Email Notifications

Email notifications are sent automatically for:
- Quote approval (with booking details)
- Quote rejection (with reason)
- Status updates (if applicable)

**To verify email was sent:**
1. Check customer's email inbox
2. Ask customer to check spam/junk folder
3. If not received, contact Developer

### Notification Settings

To customize which notifications are sent:

**Step 1: Go to Settings**

Click **"Settings"** in left sidebar.

**Step 2: Click Notifications**

Click **"Notifications"** tab.

**Step 3: Configure Preferences**

Choose which events trigger notifications:
- ☑ Quote approved
- ☑ Quote rejected
- ☑ Booking scheduled
- ☑ Status updates

**Step 4: Choose Notification Type**

For each event, select:
- Email only
- SMS only
- Both email and SMS

**Step 5: Save**

Click **"Save Settings"**

Changes take effect immediately.

## Module 4: Responding to Contacts

### Overview

Customers can submit contact forms from the website with questions, inquiries, or feedback. You need to review these and respond appropriately.

### Step-by-Step: Viewing Contact Submissions

**Step 1: Navigate to Contact Submissions**

Click **"Contact Submissions"** in the left sidebar.

You'll see a list of all submissions with:
- **Name** - Customer name
- **Email** - Customer email
- **Phone** - Customer phone
- **Date** - Submission date
- **Message Preview** - First part of message

**Step 2: Sort and Filter**

You can sort by:
- **Date** - Newest first or oldest first
- **Status** - Responded or Not Responded

Click column headers to sort.

### Step-by-Step: Responding to a Submission

**Step 1: Click on Submission**

Click on a contact submission to view the full message.

You'll see:
- Customer name and contact info
- Full message text
- Submission date
- Response status

**Step 2: Read the Message**

Carefully read what the customer is asking or saying.

Common submission types:
- **Service Inquiry** - Customer asking about services
- **Scheduling Request** - Customer wants to book
- **Complaint** - Customer reporting an issue
- **Partnership** - Business inquiry
- **Other** - General inquiry

**Step 3: Determine Response Type**

Based on the message, decide how to respond:

**For Service Inquiries:**
- Provide service details
- Include pricing information
- Direct to booking page
- Offer to answer more questions

**For Scheduling Requests:**
- Direct to online booking system
- Or schedule directly if preferred
- Provide available dates/times
- Confirm details

**For Complaints:**
- Apologize for the issue
- Investigate the problem
- Offer solution or compensation
- Follow up to verify satisfaction

**For Partnership Inquiries:**
- Thank them for interest
- Provide company contact info
- Forward to owner if needed
- Set up meeting if appropriate

**Step 4: Respond to Customer**

You have two options:

**Option A: Email Response**
1. Click **"Reply via Email"**
2. Type your response
3. Click **"Send"**

**Option B: Phone Response**
1. Note the phone number
2. Call customer at convenient time
3. Mark as responded when done

**Step 5: Mark as Responded**

After responding, click **"Mark as Responded"**

This removes it from your to-do list and tracks that you've handled it.

### Response Best Practices

**Be Professional**
- Use proper grammar and spelling
- Be courteous and friendly
- Stay professional in tone

**Be Clear**
- Answer their specific question
- Provide all needed information
- Include next steps

**Be Timely**
- Respond within 24 hours
- Acknowledge receipt if you need time to research
- Follow up if promised

**Be Helpful**
- Go above and beyond if possible
- Offer solutions, not excuses
- Make it easy for them to book

## Common Workflows

### Workflow 1: Daily Morning Routine

**Time: 9:00 AM**

1. Log in to admin dashboard
2. Check Dashboard widget for overnight bookings
3. Review any new contact submissions
4. Check SMS logs for delivery issues
5. Note any equipment that needs attention

**Action Items:**
- Approve/reject any awaiting bookings
- Respond to urgent contact submissions
- Update inventory if needed

### Workflow 2: Processing a Quote Request

**Time: Throughout day**

1. Customer submits quote request on website
2. You receive notification (email/SMS)
3. Go to Bookings section
4. Find new booking (status: Awaiting Review)
5. Review all details
6. Approve or reject with notes
7. Customer receives notification
8. Customer can now see status in portal

**Timeline:** 5-10 minutes per booking

### Workflow 3: Updating Inventory After Job

**Time: After each job**

1. Complete service for customer
2. Return equipment to base
3. Log in to admin dashboard
4. Go to Inventory
5. Update quantities (e.g., 5 containers → 4 if one is in repair)
6. Add note explaining change
7. Mark any damaged equipment as Inactive
8. Order replacements if needed

**Timeline:** 2-3 minutes per update

### Workflow 4: Responding to Customer Inquiry

**Time: As received**

1. Customer submits contact form
2. You receive notification
3. Go to Contact Submissions
4. Read full message
5. Determine response type
6. Reply via email or phone
7. Mark as Responded
8. Follow up if needed

**Timeline:** 5-15 minutes per inquiry

### Workflow 5: Weekly Inventory Check

**Time: Friday afternoon**

1. Go to Inventory section
2. Count physical equipment
3. Compare to system quantities
4. Update any discrepancies
5. Note any items needing repair
6. Plan purchases for next week
7. Review low-stock items

**Timeline:** 20-30 minutes
